The whole day, in ragas

These are the traditional pairings. They're a guide, not a rule, so listen whenever you like.

Early morningBhairav · Ahir Bhairav
MorningTodi · Bilaskhani Todi
Late morningBrindavani Sarang · Madhyamad Sarang
AfternoonBhimpalasi · Multani
DuskPuriya Dhanashree · Marwa
EveningYaman · Bihag · Kedar
NightDarbari Kanada · Malkauns · Bageshri
